A paragraph is a group of sentences that develop one main idea. It typically begins with a topic sentence that introduces the main point, followed by supporting sentences that provide details, examples, or evidence. The paragraph concludes with a sentence that either summarizes the main idea or provides a final thought related to it, creating a cohesive unit of thought.
